Meet Webling

A tiny, friendly spider who hangs out on your web pages. He swings, chats, cheers you on — and never gets in the way of what you're doing.

Alt

Summon him on any page

Summon, drag & play

Press the shortcut or hit Summon in the toolbar popup. Drag Webling anywhere, click him to swing, or focus him with Tab and use the arrow keys.

FOCUS24:59

A calm timer that keeps running when you close the popup

Focus sessions

Start a 15–60 minute session (or any length up to 3 hours). Webling quiets down, shows a small timer on the page and on the toolbar icon, then takes a break with you.

💧 WaterRescues🏆 Rewards

Water, rescues & rewards

Log glasses of water, rescue the tiny spiders that dangle by now and then, and unlock new colors, accessories and web lines as you go.

🛡️
  • No accounts
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No servers — everything stays in your browser

Private by design

Hide Webling on any site with one click, snooze him for a while, or switch him off entirely from the popup.
